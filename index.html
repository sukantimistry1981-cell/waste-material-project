About Our Science Project

This project explains how waste materials can be reused to create useful products. Reuse and recycling help reduce pollution and protect our environment.

♻️ Creative Project Ideas

1. Plastic Bottle Planter

Reuse an empty plastic bottle to grow small plants.

2. Newspaper Paper Bag

Turn old newspapers into useful paper bags.

3. Cardboard Organizer

Reuse cardboard boxes to organize stationery.

4. Kitchen Waste Compost

Convert suitable fruit and vegetable scraps into compost.

🌱 Benefits

  • Reduces waste and pollution
  • Saves natural resources
  • Encourages creativity
  • Promotes a cleaner environment
